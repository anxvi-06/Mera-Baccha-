Dear Mera Bachha,

I don't think I say it enough, but thank you.

Thank you for being there for me, especially during the moments when everything feels difficult. Somehow, you have this ridiculous little superpower of making heavy days feel lighter.

You make me laugh when I don't even feel like smiling. You make ordinary moments feel special. You listen to me, you care about me, you pamper me, and somehow you still manage to be my absolute cutie afterwards.

And yes, I notice all of it.

I notice the little things. I notice how much you care. I notice how scared you are of losing me. I notice every time you try to make me happy.

I don't think you realise how much those things mean to me.

I am genuinely grateful that you are in my life. I want you around for the stupid conversations, the random laughter, the difficult days, the good days, and all the completely ordinary moments in between.

Because somehow, when you're there, difficult things don't feel quite as difficult.

So this is my unnecessarily dramatic way of saying:

Thank you for being you. Thank you for choosing to care. And thank you for being my Mera Bachha.
