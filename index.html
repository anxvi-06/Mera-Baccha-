I don't think I say it enough, but thank you.

Thank you for being there for me, especially during the moments when everything feels difficult. Somehow, you have this real power of making heaviness feel lighter.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for being there with me even when I act like a whole stubborn baby. And thank you for still being here after so many fights, arguments, and all the unnecessary drama I somehow manage to create.

You make me laugh even when I don't feel like smiling. You make me blush like hell. You literally turn me into a red tomato like crazy, dude. And somehow, you still find that cute.

Then you listen to me.

You listen to all my rants. You listen to things you don't even relate to. You listen when I have absolutely nothing sensible to say, and somehow you still stay and hear me out.

And then you pamper me.

Even after all those unnecessary "sorrys" that I pretend I'm tired of — okay, maybe I am a little tired of them — I still love you a lot.

And yes, I notice all of it.

I notice the little things. I notice how much you care. I notice how you try to make me happy, how you try to protect me, how you worry about me, and how you keep trying even when I make things unnecessarily difficult.

I know I sometimes fight over stupid things. I know I can be stubborn for absolutely no reason. But thank you for being patient with me anyway.

I don't think you realise how much those little things mean to me.

More than big gifts. More than anything expensive. I don't need those things.

It's the little things that stay with me.

The way you listen. The way you care. The way you try. The way you make me laugh when I don't want to. The way you somehow manage to make even an ordinary day feel a little better just by being there.

I'm genuinely grateful that you're in my life.

I'm grateful for the random laughter, the difficult days, the good days, the stupid conversations, the unnecessary fights, the apologies, the teasing, the little moments, and everything in between.

I'm grateful for you.

And honestly, you are one of the best things that has ever happened to me.

So thank you.

For choosing me.
For staying.
For caring.
For listening.
For making me laugh.
For making me blush like an idiot.
For pampering me.
For putting up with my stubbornness.
And most importantly, for being you.

I hope you always know that I notice.

I hope you always know that I appreciate you.

And I hope you always know that you mean so much more to me than I probably know how to say.

— your baby ♡
